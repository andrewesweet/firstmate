10. Missing, failed, or unhealthy cycle only: if a later notification explicitly reports one of those repair conditions, drain queued wakes, inspect the failure text, call `fm_watch_arm_omp`, and exit omp and relaunch through `fm <project>` if the extensions are not loaded.
